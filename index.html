About this portfolio

These tools were developed to demonstrate hands-on technical readiness for the GEMS Digital M&E Specialist role. All three KoboToolbox forms are live and deployable. The MIS dashboard, TLM Track & Trace, and GEMS field form simulations illustrate the full data collection pipeline — from field submission to management dashboard. The Papua New Guinea context reflects real programme experience on the LEAP project (P505529).

LEAP PNG — Management Information System Dashboard

Full MIS dashboard covering all 4 project components and PDO indicators across 8 target provinces. Built around the LEAP PNG Results Framework (P505529). Data Collection Pipeline — How Data Enters the MIS

Three-tab simulation answering the question: How was this data collected? Demonstrates the full pipeline from field to dashboard across three tools: Bluetree Track & Trace for TLM distribution (with interactive carton scan interface), GEMS field forms for classroom construction verification (GPS-tagged photos, works checklist), and GEMS multi-contractor ICT hub refurbishment forms (5 contractors, one visit).
