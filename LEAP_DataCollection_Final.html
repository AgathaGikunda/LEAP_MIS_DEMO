💻 How GEMS feeds the MIS dashboard — ICT Hub Refurbishment The field officer visits the school once and assesses all five contractors in a single GEMS form submission. Each contractor section has its own status and photo evidence. The auto-calculated components complete score (0–5) determines the hub's overall status on the dashboard map. A hub is only marked Complete when all 5 components are confirmed.
